About The Margins

I’m Jerry Zou. The Margins is my ongoing journal about displacement and the places people try to make a home.

Why I keep this journal

I grew up in Shanghai, Hong Kong and Singapore and now study in California. Through Church World Service, I have helped look for housing for refugee families in Long Beach. My interests in engineering and displacement also meet in a collaboration on water treatment.

Those experiences shape the questions I follow here. A rental listing can appear affordable until a call reveals how the landlord calculates income. A water-treatment device can work in a laboratory while questions about maintenance and everyday use remain. I want to understand what it takes for an offer of help to become something people can use.

The journal also gives me a place to follow questions beyond my own work: how sand extraction changes a coastline, or what finding a suitable home involves after a fire. Some pieces begin with something I have done or noticed; others begin with a report I want to understand more closely.

I’ll keep adding to this journal as that work develops, including shorter observations and questions I haven’t yet resolved.
