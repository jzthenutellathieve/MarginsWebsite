About The Margins

The Margins is a shared project documenting displacement and the places people call home, through articles, field notes and work on housing and water access.

How it started

The project began with Jerry Zou’s interest in land reclamation in Singapore. Where does the sand for a new coastline come from? What happens to the places it leaves behind?

Following those questions led to a broader interest in displacement: why people lose a place to live, and what it takes to settle somewhere new. The writing grew to include housing searches with refugee families in Long Beach, resettlement after a fire in Hong Kong, and questions about access to water.

The Margins is open to other people’s observations, too. Contributors share photographs and short field notes from the places around them. A note can become the starting point for a longer piece, or stay as a small record of a place and a moment. The aim is to keep building this record together.

Special thanks

Thank you to Xingwei Wang, Jerry’s research mentor, for his guidance and help with Water by Hand, which they co-authored.

The underlying TEHG-UV device and laboratory experiments are the work of Wang and his research colleagues. Jerry’s contribution concerns its possible use after displacement and the information a humanitarian organization would need to consider a trial. The water project explains that work in more detail.
