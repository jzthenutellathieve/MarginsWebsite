Housing Navigation

Rental searches in Long Beach: rent, deposits, income requirements and other conditions families need to check.

September 2026: CWS Orange County has announced the closure of its office. When Federal Funding Cuts Reach Refugees’ Everyday Lives follows the policy and funding changes cited in that announcement and the questions about continuing help for families still looking for housing.

A rental search involves more than the advertised rent. Deposits, income checks, credit requirements and voucher policies can determine whether a family can apply.

Author’s note — Jerry Zou

As a volunteer with CWS Orange County, I found rental listings, called landlords and organized the answers for case managers. I record rent and bedroom count, along with deposits, income requirements, credit checks and whether housing vouchers are accepted. Those details help show which listings are worth following up.

One property I contacted used its market rent to calculate the income requirement, even though the advertised rent was lower. I recorded both figures so a case manager could see how the requirement was calculated. Other properties need to be checked individually.

Keeping the records useful

Future updates will focus on when a landlord last confirmed a requirement, what still needs checking, and which details case managers find useful. An anonymized example of the search records is planned for this page.
