Our Mission and History

The Margins is an independent journal about displacement and the places people call home. We report on how policy, development and environmental change affect where people can live, and what it takes to settle again after leaving or losing a home.

The Margins began in 2024 with writing and photographs about Singapore’s sand trade and land reclamation, alongside observations from Hong Kong and Singapore. Through 2025, Jerry Zou and his classmates continued documenting the places around them and the changes they encountered. The project took its current name in 2026.

Today, The Margins reports on displacement in communities around the world. Alongside articles by editor-in-chief Xingtong Jerry Zou, our Field Notes section brings together photographs and short accounts from his classmates and members of the wider community, recording what they see where they live and travel.
