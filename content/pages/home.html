Our Mission and History

The Margins is an independent journal focused on displacement and the homes that people identify themselves with. It covers stories on how policies, development and environmental factors affect people’s ability to stay in place, and what should be done for people to be able to return home.

The Margins was founded by Xingtong Jerry Zou in 2024 through articles and photos about the sand trade and land reclamation in Singapore, as well as observations in Hong Kong and Singapore. Between 2025 and now, the members and contributors have continued their project by recording observations in the places that surround them. The project took its current name "The Margins" in 2026.

Currently, The Margins gives news about displacement in communities all over the world. Apart from the long articles, Field Notes gathers photos and short stories by members of The Margins and others in the community about what they see in their surroundings.
